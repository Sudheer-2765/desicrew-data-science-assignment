## 2. Detailed Section Reference

### 2.1 Employee Support Policy (`employee_support_policy.md`)
- **Section 1.1: Support Channels**  
  Details ServiceHub portal access (`https://servicehub.northstar.internal`), designated Slack triage channels (`#help-it-support`, `#help-hr-operations`, `#help-payroll-finance`), and emergency helpline contacts (`ext. 4400`).
- **Section 1.2: Operating Hours**  
  Outlines standard IT helpdesk hours (Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM IST), 24x7x365 on-call support for P1 critical incidents, and physical campus kiosk schedules (9:30 AM – 5:30 PM IST).
- **Section 1.3: Service Level Agreements (SLA) and Response Times**  
  Defines acknowledgment and resolution target SLAs across four severity tiers: P1 Critical (15 min ack / <2 hr fix), P2 High (1 hr ack / <8 hr fix), P3 Medium (4 hr ack / <24 hr fix), and P4 Low (8 hr ack / <48 hr fix).
- **Section 2.1: Escalation Matrix**  
  Outlines the four-tier escalation ladder: L1 Incident Handler, L2 Team Lead (`it-escalations@northstar.internal`), L3 Operations Manager (`ops-lead@northstar.internal`), and L4 VP of Infrastructure (`vp-workplace@northstar.internal`).
- **Section 2.2: IT and Helpdesk Services**  
  Governs standard engineering laptop hardware allocations, 36-month hardware refresh cycles, peripheral replacement procedures, and software catalog compliance.
- **Section 2.3: Human Resources and People Operations Inquiries**  
  Covers confidential HR ticket handling, group health insurance & benefits inquiries (`benefits@northstar.internal`), and Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) grievance channels (`posh@northstar.internal`).
- **Section 2.4: Access Control and Security Credentials**  
  Covers Okta SSO, mandatory FIDO2/authenticator MFA, 16-character password guidelines, Just-In-Time (JIT) 8-hour privileged access leases, and account lockout recovery via video verification.

### 2.2 Leave and Attendance Policy (`leave_and_attendance_policy.md`)
- **Section 1.1: Core Working Hours**  
  Specifies 40-hour work week, mandatory core overlap hours from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST, and allowable shift bands (8:00 AM – 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM IST).
- **Section 1.2: Attendance Logging**  
  Details digital attendance logging via Northstar PeoplePulse, campus turnstile biometric integration, and the 10:15 AM check-in cut-off.
- **Section 2.1: Leave Categories and Annual Entitlements**  
  Comprehensive table detailing Earned Leave (18 days), Sick Leave (12 days), Casual Leave (6 days), Maternity Leave (26 weeks), Paternity Leave (15 days), Bereavement Leave (5 days), and Compensatory Off rules.
- **Section 2.2: Leave Eligibility and Accrual Rules**  
  EL accrual at 1.5 days/month, 3-month probation utilization restrictions, and year-end carry-over cap of 12 days.
- **Section 2.3: Request Process and Notice Periods**  
  Notice requirements: 14 days for planned leaves (>3 days), 48 hours for short leaves (1–3 days), and within 2 hours of shift start for emergency casual leaves.
- **Section 2.4: Managerial Approval Workflow**  
  Manager 48-hour response SLA, automatic escalation to skip-level manager, and auto-approval upon continued inaction.
- **Section 3.1: Sick Leave and Medical Documentation**  
  Self-declaration for 1–2 days; mandatory registered medical practitioner certificate required for 3+ consecutive days, due within 48 hours of return.
- **Section 3.2: Remote Work and Work-From-Home (WFH) Guidelines**  
  Hybrid baseline of up to 2 days remote work per week, 50 Mbps network prerequisite, and director approval for temporary remote work outside primary location.
- **Section 3.3: Late Arrival and Unplanned Absence**  
  Allowance of 3 monthly grace late check-ins, half-day deduction starting from the 4th occurrence, and 3 consecutive unnotified days classified as job abandonment.
- **Section 4.1: Policy Escalations and Grievances**  
  5-day appeal window to People Business Partners (`hr-bp@northstar.internal`) for disputed leave denials, and up to 10 days of discretionary Special Paid Leave for extraordinary events.

### 2.3 Reimbursement Policy (`reimbursement_policy.md`)
- **Section 1.1: Eligible Expense Categories**  
  Official travel transit, authorized lodging, client meals and entertainment, local business cabs/mileage, monthly internet stipend, and professional learning allowances.
- **Section 1.2: Non-Reimbursable Exclusions**  
  Fines and parking violations, alcohol/tobacco without executive approval, cabin class travel upgrades, companion costs, personal grooming/spa, and credit card late finance fees.
- **Section 2.1: Spending Limits and Daily Caps**  
  City tier allowance ceilings: Tier-1 hotels up to ₹6,500/night; Tier-2 up to ₹4,500/night; meals up to ₹1,500/day (Tier-1) or $75/day (international); monthly internet stipend of ₹1,500; annual learning allowance of ₹35,000.
- **Section 2.2: Receipt and Documentation Requirements**  
  Mandatory itemized tax/GST invoice for claims exceeding ₹200, inadequacy of credit card slips alone, ₹1,000 missing receipt limit, and strict 30-day submission cutoff.
- **Section 3.1: Expense Submission Workflow**  
  Step-by-step submission process in ExpenseDesk portal (`https://expensedesk.northstar.internal`), digital receipt uploads, and tracking ID generation (`EXP-2026-XXXX`).
- **Section 3.2: Approval Hierarchies**  
  Approval thresholds: Tier 1 (up to ₹25,000) by Manager; Tier 2 (up to ₹1,00,000) by Dept Head; Tier 3 (>₹1,00,000) by Corporate Controller / CFO; 5 business days approver turnaround SLA.
- **Section 3.3: Payout and Settlement Timelines**  
  Finance audit within 4 business days; semi-monthly disbursement runs on the 15th and 30th directly to payroll bank accounts.
- **Section 4.1: Travel, Meals, and Local Conveyance Rules**  
  14-day advance booking requirement for airfare via Travel Desk (`travel@northstar.internal`), personal mileage rates (₹12/km car, ₹6/km two-wheeler), and highest-ranking attendee billing rule for group meals.
- **Section 4.2: Audit and Dispute Escalations**  
  Random post-payment compliance audits within 90 days, salary recovery for fraudulent claims, and 7-day appeal process via `expense-audit@northstar.internal`.

## 3. Citation Guidelines for the Support Assistant

When generating responses, the Assistant must follow this standardized citation pattern:
- **Format:** `[Document Title, Section X.Y]` or `[Document Title, Section X.Y: Title]`
- **Examples:**
  - *According to company policy, planned leaves exceeding 3 days require 14 calendar days advance notice [Leave and Attendance Policy, Section 2.3].*
  - *Home broadband costs can be claimed up to ₹1,500 per month [Reimbursement Policy, Section 2.1].*
  - *Critical P1 incidents carry an initial response target of 15 minutes and resolution within 2 hours [Employee Support Policy, Section 1.3].*
