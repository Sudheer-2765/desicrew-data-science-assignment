**Applies To:** All full-time employees, probationers, and retainers across Northstar Technologies

## 1. Working Hours and Attendance Management

### Section 1.1: Core Working Hours
Northstar Technologies maintains a flexible yet synchronized working arrangement designed to balance personal productivity with collaborative team overlap:

1. **Standard Working Hours:** A full-time work week consists of 40 scheduled hours, typically structured as 8 working hours per day plus a 1-hour meal break (total 9 hours on-site or online), Monday through Friday.
2. **Core Collaboration Hours:** To facilitate cross-team standups, architectural reviews, and client engagements, all team members are required to be online and available between **10:00 AM and 4:00 PM Indian Standard Time (IST)**.
3. **Flexible Bands:** Employees may flex their shift start between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM IST, and their shift end between 5:00 PM and 7:00 PM IST, subject to squad lead agreement and project requirements.

### Section 1.2: Attendance Logging
Accurate attendance tracking is essential for operational planning and statutory compliance:

1. **Digital Check-In Mechanism:** Employees log daily check-ins and check-outs via the **Northstar PeoplePulse Portal** or the mobile PeoplePulse application.
2. **Campus Biometric Verification:** When working from physical campuses (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune), badge swipes at turnstile security readers automatically synchronize with PeoplePulse.
3. **Check-In Timelines:** Daily check-in must occur no later than 10:15 AM IST. Failure to register attendance by 10:30 AM without an approved leave record automatically triggers an automated Slack notification to the employee and direct supervisor.

## 2. Leave Entitlements and Application Lifecycle

### Section 2.1: Leave Categories and Annual Entitlements
Northstar Technologies provides a comprehensive leave basket structured as follows:

| Leave Type | Annual Allotment | Carry-Over Limit | Encashment on Exit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Earned Leave (EL) / Paid Time Off** | 18 business days | Up to 12 days to next calendar year | Eligible (max 30 days) |
| **Sick / Medical Leave (SL)** | 12 business days | Does not carry over (lapses Dec 31) | Not eligible |
| **Casual Leave (CL)** | 6 business days | Does not carry over (lapses Dec 31) | Not eligible |
| **Maternity Leave** | 26 consecutive weeks | N/A | Statutory |
| **Paternity Leave** | 15 business days | Must be utilized within 6 months | Not eligible |
| **Bereavement Leave** | 5 business days per event | Immediate family (parents, spouse, child, sibling) | Not eligible |
| **Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)** | As earned | Must be consumed within 60 calendar days | Not eligible |

### Section 2.2: Leave Eligibility and Accrual Rules
1. **Accrual Schedule:** Earned Leave (EL) accrues pro-rata at the rate of 1.5 business days on the first calendar day of each completed month of service. Sick Leave (12 days) and Casual Leave (6 days) are credited in full at the beginning of each calendar year (January 1).
2. **Probationary Period:** New hires during their initial 3-month probation period may utilize accrued Casual Leave and Sick Leave. Earned Leave can be accrued during probation but may only be taken after successful confirmation of employment, unless granted as an exception by the Head of HR.
3. **Year-End Carry-Forward:** A maximum of 12 unused Earned Leave days can be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year. Any excess EL balance beyond 12 days lapses automatically on December 31 at 11:59 PM.

### Section 2.3: Request Process and Notice Periods
Leave requests must be submitted through the PeoplePulse Portal according to the following mandatory notice windows:

1. **Planned Leaves (EL exceeding 3 consecutive days):** Must be requested at least **14 calendar days** in advance to allow resource and sprint balancing.
2. **Short Leaves (EL or CL of 1 to 3 days):** Must be requested at least **48 hours** in advance.
3. **Emergency Casual Leave:** In cases of unforeseen personal emergencies, notice must be logged in PeoplePulse or communicated to the reporting manager via Slack/email within **2 hours** of the employee's scheduled shift start.

### Section 2.4: Managerial Approval Workflow
1. **Response SLA:** Reporting managers are required to review and respond to leave requests within **48 hours** of submission.
2. **Automatic Escalation:** If a manager does not take action within 48 hours, the request automatically escalates to the skip-level manager. If no action occurs within an additional 24 hours, the ticket is auto-approved by the HRMS system to prevent disruption to employee plans.
3. **Grounds for Decline:** Managers may decline planned leave requests solely based on documented business exigencies (e.g., active production deployment freeze, critical contract milestone), and must provide written feedback with alternative dates.

## 3. Sick Leave, Remote Work, and Absences

### Section 3.1: Sick Leave and Medical Documentation
1. **Short-Term Illness:** Sick Leave taken for 1 or 2 consecutive working days requires no medical certificate; self-declaration via PeoplePulse is sufficient.
2. **Extended Medical Absence:** Any sick leave extending beyond **2 consecutive working days** (i.e., 3 or more days) requires an official medical fitness and sickness certificate signed by a Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP).
3. **Submission Deadline:** The medical certificate must be scanned and uploaded to PeoplePulse within **48 hours** of returning to active duties. Failure to supply a valid certificate results in the duration being treated as Loss of Pay (LOP).

### Section 3.2: Remote Work and Work-From-Home (WFH) Guidelines
Northstar Technologies champions a hybrid workplace policy:

1. **Standard Hybrid Cadence:** Full-time employees are eligible for up to **2 days of remote work per week**, with remaining days spent at an assigned Northstar campus or co-working space.
2. **Technical Pre-requisites:** Remote employees must possess an active broadband connection with minimum 50 Mbps download/upload speeds and a distraction-free home office setup.
3. **Work Outside Primary Location:** Short-term remote work outside an employee's registered state or metropolitan hub (up to 3 weeks per calendar year) requires prior approval from the engineering director and HR partner to ensure tax and labor compliance. International remote work is strictly prohibited without executive leadership authorization.

### Section 3.3: Late Arrival and Unplanned Absence
1. **Grace Arrivals:** Employees are granted up to **3 grace late arrivals** per calendar month (defined as check-in between 10:15 AM and 10:45 AM IST).
2. **Excessive Lateness:** A 4th late check-in within the same calendar month will result in a mandatory deduction of a half-day Casual Leave or Loss of Pay (LOP) if CL balance is zero.
3. **Absconding / Abandonment of Service:** Unnotified absence spanning **3 consecutive business days** without direct communication to the manager or HR will be classified as job abandonment. The company reserves the right to initiate termination and asset recovery procedures.

## 4. Governance and Escalations

### Section 4.1: Policy Escalations and Grievances
1. **Arbitrary Leave Denials:** Employees who feel their leave requests were unfairly rejected or revoked may appeal to their People Business Partner (`hr-bp@northstar.internal`) within **5 business days** of denial.
2. **Special Leave Relief:** In exceptional life events (e.g., natural disasters, terminal family illness), the Employee Welfare Committee may grant up to 10 additional days of Special Paid Discretionary Leave upon written recommendation by the VP of People.
