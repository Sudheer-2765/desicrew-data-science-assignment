# Northstar Technologies — Expense and Reimbursement Policy

**Document Reference:** NST-POL-EXP-2026-V4  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Revised:** March 1, 2026  
**Custodian:** Finance, Travel, & Corporate Controller Group  
**Applies To:** All permanent staff, contract associates, and business travelers at Northstar Technologies  

---

## 1. Scope and Eligible Expenses

### Section 1.1: Eligible Expense Categories
Northstar Technologies reimburses legitimate, direct, and reasonable expenses incurred by employees exclusively in the performance of official business. Reimbursable categories comprise:

1. **Official Business Travel:** Economy commercial flights, intercity passenger trains (AC 2-Tier / Chair Car), intercity bus transport, and toll/parking charges during business transit.
2. **Lodging and Accommodations:** Hotel stays booked in accordance with company tier allowances during authorized corporate travel.
3. **Meals and Business Entertainment:** Daily subsistence during business trips and business development dining with active clients or external partners.
4. **Local Conveyance:** App-based taxi aggregators (e.g., Uber, Ola), authorized radio cabs, auto-rickshaws, or personal vehicle mileage incurred when traveling to client offices, offsite data centers, or transit hubs.
5. **Remote Connectivity Stipend:** Monthly home internet and cellular telecommunications reimbursement for designated hybrid and remote workers.
6. **Continuous Professional Learning:** Pre-approved professional certification fees, technical conference passes, and technical publication subscriptions.

### Section 1.2: Non-Reimbursable Exclusions
The company will strictly not reimburse personal, avoidable, or non-compliant expenses, including but not limited to:

1. **Traffic and Parking Violations:** Fines, speeding tickets, wheel clamp penalties, or towing charges.
2. **Alcohol and Tobacco:** Alcoholic beverages, minibar snacks, tobacco, and leisure club expenses unless an explicit executive exception was pre-approved in writing for an authorized client event.
3. **Travel Upgrades:** Flight seat selection fees, premium economy/business class cabin upgrades, priority boarding surcharges, and flight change fees due to employee negligence.
4. **Family and Companion Costs:** Hotel charges, transport, meals, or visa fees attributable to accompanying spouses, children, or non-employee companions.
5. **Personal Care and Fitness:** Hotel spa, gymnasium, dry cleaning for trips under 4 business days, personal grooming, and toiletries.
6. **Financial Penalties:** Late payment interest, finance charges, or currency exchange penalty fees on personal credit cards.

## 2. Limits, Caps, and Receipt Standards

### Section 2.1: Spending Limits and Daily Caps
All reimbursement claims are subject to fixed monetary ceilings established by city tier classifications (Tier-1: Bengaluru, Mumbai, NCR-Delhi, Hyderabad; Tier-2: Pune, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad; Tier-3: All other municipal districts):

| Expense Type | Tier-1 City Limit | Tier-2 City Limit | Tier-3 City Limit | International Daily Cap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hotel Accommodation (per night)** | Up to ₹6,500 + taxes | Up to ₹4,500 + taxes | Up to ₹3,000 + taxes | Up to $180 / €160 + taxes |
| **Daily Meals & Incidentals (per day)** | Up to ₹1,500 | Up to ₹1,000 | Up to ₹750 | Up to $75 / €70 |
| **Remote Internet Stipend** | ₹1,500 per month | ₹1,500 per month | ₹1,500 per month | N/A |
| **Annual Learning Allowance** | Up to ₹35,000 / fiscal year | Up to ₹35,000 / fiscal year | Up to ₹35,000 / fiscal year | Up to $500 / fiscal year |

*Note: Any expense exceeding the stated caps requires prior written pre-approval from the functional Vice President prior to expense commitment.*

### Section 2.2: Receipt and Documentation Requirements
Northstar requires stringent substantiation for all expense disclosures:

1. **Original Tax Invoices:** All claims exceeding **₹200** (or local currency equivalent) must be accompanied by an itemized tax invoice or GST-registered vendor cash receipt displaying vendor name, invoice date, itemized description, and tax breakdown.
2. **Card Transaction Slips:** Credit card swipe receipts or digital wallet transfer screenshots that omit itemized product details are insufficient on their own and will be rejected.
3. **Lost Receipt Declaration:** For small expenditures under ₹200 (such as street transit or quick tolls) where receipts are unobtainable, a digital missing-receipt statutory declaration may be filed, capped at a maximum of ₹1,000 per multi-day trip.
4. **Submission Window:** Expenses must be submitted within **30 calendar days** from the date the transaction occurred. Claims submitted after 30 days are automatically closed and will not be reimbursed without CFO approval.

## 3. Workflow, Approvals, and Payouts

### Section 3.1: Expense Submission Workflow
Employees process reimbursement claims through the **Northstar ExpenseDesk Portal (`https://expensedesk.northstar.internal`)**:

1. **Digital Expense Report Creation:** The claimant initiates a new Expense Report, selects the appropriate project billing code, and enters date-ordered itemized lines.
2. **Receipt Attachment:** High-resolution scans or photos (PDF, PNG, JPEG under 10 MB per file) of all itemized bills must be attached to the respective expense entries.
3. **Submission and Tracking:** Once submitted, ExpenseDesk generates a unique claim tracking ID (e.g., `EXP-2026-XXXX`) and routes the package into the multi-tier approval queue.

### Section 3.2: Approval Hierarchies
Expense approvals are gated strictly by cumulative claim value:

- **Tier 1 (Up to ₹25,000):** Direct Reporting Manager approval only.
- **Tier 2 (₹25,001 to ₹1,00,000):** Reporting Manager approval followed by Department Head (Director / Senior Director).
- **Tier 3 (Above ₹1,00,000 or any International Travel):** Reporting Manager, Department Head, and Corporate Finance Controller / CFO.
- **Turnaround SLA:** Approvers are expected to approve or reject reports within **5 business days** of receipt.

### Section 3.3: Payout and Settlement Timelines
1. **Audit Processing:** The Finance Audit Team audits approved expense packages within **4 business days** of final manager sign-off.
2. **Disbursement Schedule:** Approved reimbursements are settled twice per calendar month:
   - Claims cleared on or before the 12th are credited on the **15th of the month**.
   - Claims cleared on or before the 27th are credited on the **30th (or final business day) of the month**.
3. **Payment Method:** Direct electronic bank deposit into the employee's registered corporate salary bank account, with an itemized payment advice slip emailed simultaneously.

## 4. Specialized Travel Rules and Auditing

### Section 4.1: Travel, Meals, and Local Conveyance Rules
1. **Air Travel Bookings:** All flights must be booked through the Northstar Corporate Travel Desk (`travel@northstar.internal`) at least **14 calendar days** in advance. Non-emergency direct self-bookings outside the travel desk will not be refunded.
2. **Mileage Reimbursement for Personal Vehicles:** Employees using personal vehicles for approved business journeys are reimbursed at:
   - Four-wheeler (Car): **₹12.00 per kilometer**
   - Two-wheeler (Motorcycle/Scooter): **₹6.00 per kilometer**  
   *Claimants must log starting location, destination odometer readings, and Google Maps route confirmations.*
3. **Group and Client Dining:** When dining with multiple Northstar colleagues, the highest-ranking executive present must settle and submit the expense claim. The receipt must enumerate the full names and company affiliations of all attendees.

### Section 4.2: Audit and Dispute Escalations
1. **Post-Payment Audits:** Finance reserves the right to conduct random compliance audits within 90 days post-settlement. If a claim is discovered to be fraudulent, inflated, or non-compliant, the reimbursed amount will be deducted from the subsequent month's salary, and disciplinary review will ensue.
2. **Disputed Claims Resolution:** If a legitimate claim is rejected by the audit desk, the employee may raise an appeal within **7 business days** to `expense-audit@northstar.internal` providing additional business justification.
